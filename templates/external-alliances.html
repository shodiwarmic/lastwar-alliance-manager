Search your registry, or look up any alliance on lastrank.fun — across every server, since VS Duel League opponents usually aren't on ours. A tag often exists on many servers, so check the server number before picking. You can also paste a lastrank.fun alliance link directly. The report is not saved — it lives in this tab until you leave. If the alliance is already in your registry, its power, kills and member count are refreshed.
